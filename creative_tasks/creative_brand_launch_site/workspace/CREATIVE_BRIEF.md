# Brief: The Nocturne Archive

Design the launch site for an imaginary after-dark cultural venue that combines
an experimental library, listening room, and late-night lecture hall. The site
must introduce the venue, feature three opening-week events, communicate the
phrase **“Ideas have a nightlife”**, and invite visitors to reserve a free
membership. Avoid familiar luxury-black-and-gold, generic neon cyberpunk, and
standard startup landing-page aesthetics. The personality should be cerebral,
welcoming, surprising, and unmistakably nocturnal.
