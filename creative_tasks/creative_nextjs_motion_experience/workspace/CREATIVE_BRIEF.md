# Brief: Lumen Atlas

Design a cinematic Next.js product story for **Lumen Atlas**, an imaginary tool
that lets people collect the changing quality of light in meaningful places.
The experience moves between an atlas overview and individual “light memory”
details. Use shared-layout motion to make navigation feel spatial, scroll-linked
animation to reveal the product’s idea, and gesture feedback that feels calm and
precise. Include three fictional memories: Blue Hour Kitchen, Winter Platform,
and Last Light at Mesa Road.

Avoid standard SaaS gradients, floating glass cards, and excessive springiness.
The reduced-motion experience must feel deliberately designed, not merely inert.
