# Brief: Memory Weather

Build an interactive Three.js dreamscape titled **Memory Weather**. It should
feel like walking through a half-remembered climate: fragments gather, dissolve,
and reorganize in response to pointer movement or touch. Include a quiet opening,
a discoverable transformation, and a contemplative idle state. Avoid a generic
particle globe, starfield, spinning geometric logo, or stock shader demo.

The piece should remain legible on a modest laptop GPU and provide a meaningful
static or reduced-motion composition.
