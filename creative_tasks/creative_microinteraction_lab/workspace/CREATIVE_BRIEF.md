# Brief: Small Signals

Create a cohesive microinteraction laboratory called **Small Signals** for an
imaginary field-notes application. Include at least six interactions covering:
navigation, save/confirmation, mode switching, filtering, direct manipulation,
and an error or recovery moment. The motion language should evoke careful field
observation—responsive, organic, and restrained—without default bouncing,
gratuitous confetti, or copied component-library patterns.

Each interaction needs a short on-page caption explaining what feedback it
communicates. The full lab must work with keyboard and reduced-motion settings.
