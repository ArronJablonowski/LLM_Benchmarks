# Brief: Soft Collision Festival

Create campaign key art for **Soft Collision**, a speculative one-night music
festival where acoustic chamber ensembles collaborate with machine-generated
rhythms. The phrase **“Human pulse / synthetic weather”** must appear. The work
should express intimacy meeting vast computational scale without using robots,
circuit-board clichés, human faces, or generic waveform imagery.

Create a primary portrait artwork suitable for a 24×36 poster and a square
social adaptation. Preserve editable source where the chosen medium allows it.
