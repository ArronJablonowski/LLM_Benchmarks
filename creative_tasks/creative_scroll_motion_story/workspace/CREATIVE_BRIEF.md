# Brief: A River Remembers

Create a scroll-driven visual essay about a fictional urban river being uncovered
after a century beneath streets. Tell the story in five movements: burial,
pressure, first light, return, and stewardship. Use the recurring line **“The
city forgot the river. The river did not forget the city.”** The ending should
turn spectacle into a quiet invitation to care.

Avoid a conventional card stack, parallax-for-its-own-sake, and generic nature
stock imagery. Motion must carry meaning and remain coherent without animation.
